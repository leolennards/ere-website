# ERE website: owner's guide

This guide explains how to run the website day to day. You don't need to edit any code.

## Signing in

1. Go to **www.elevaterealestate.co.za/admin** (bookmark it).
2. Enter your email and password, then choose **Sign in**.
3. After 5 wrong passwords the account pauses for 15 minutes. That's a safety feature.
4. To change your password: **My account → Change password**. If you forget it, your web developer can reset it.

Always choose **Sign out** (bottom left) on shared computers.

## The dashboard

The **Dashboard** shows live numbers: published listings, drafts, unread and new enquiries, open valuation requests, and follow-ups that are due. Click any number to see the matching list. **Setup status** shows which optional services (maps, email alerts) are switched on.

## Adding a property

1. **Properties → New property.**
2. Fill in the title, transaction (for sale / to rent), type, price and suburb. Leave the price empty to show "Price on application". **Leave anything you don't know empty.** The website never shows "0 bedrooms".
3. Choose the **Status**. New listings start as **Draft**, which only staff can see.
4. Choose **Create listing**. You're taken to the listing page in the dashboard.
5. **Add photos.** Choose several at once. The first photo is the cover. Use ↑ ↓ to reorder or **Make cover**. Type a short description for each photo (e.g. "Open-plan kitchen with granite tops") and choose **Save**. This helps visually impaired visitors and search engines. Location data hidden in phone photos is removed automatically.
6. Check it with **Preview**.
7. Choose **Publish**. It's now live on Buy or Rent.

### Map privacy

Under *Location & map* choose:
- **Approximate**: shows the suburb only (recommended for homes).
- **Exact**: shows the street address or coordinates you entered.
- **Hidden**: no map.

The street address is never shown publicly unless you choose *Exact*.

### Featuring on the homepage

Tick **Feature on the homepage**. Up to five featured, published listings appear there. If none are featured, the homepage shows a friendly "new listings are being prepared" message.

### When a property sells or lets

Use the quick actions at the top of the listing:
- **Under offer**: still visible, with an "under offer" label (unless switched off in Settings).
- **Mark sold / Mark let**: hidden from the public by default. To show sold properties as a track record, switch it on in **Site settings → Listing visibility**, and only with the seller's permission.
- **Archive**: removes it from the website but keeps it in the dashboard.
- **Delete permanently** (owner only): you must type DELETE. Enquiries about the property are kept.

## Handling enquiries

Every form on the website (property enquiries, valuation requests, contact messages) arrives in **Enquiries**. Unread ones are bold with a red dot, and the menu shows how many are unread.

1. Open an enquiry. You'll see the person's details, how they want to be contacted, and what they asked. There are buttons to **Call**, **WhatsApp** or **Email** them.
2. After contacting them, set **Status** to *Contacted*, *In progress* or *Resolved*. Optionally choose who is **Responsible** and a **Follow up on** date, then choose **Update**.
3. Add **Internal notes** (e.g. "Viewing booked Sat 10:00"). Visitors never see these.
4. Use the filters to find enquiries by type, status, date or name, phone or reference.
5. **Export CSV** (owner only) downloads the current list for spreadsheets. Treat the file as confidential and delete it when you're done.

### Privacy requests (POPIA)

If someone asks to see their information, find their enquiry and share it with them. If they ask for it to be deleted, open the enquiry and use **Delete enquiry** (owner only; type DELETE). Once a month, delete *Resolved* enquiries older than 24 months that didn't lead to a sale. The privacy notice promises this.

## Website settings (owner only)

**Site settings** controls:
- **Business & contact:** phone, WhatsApp number (untick "Show WhatsApp buttons" if the number isn't on WhatsApp), email (only shown once you tick "verified"), address, office hours and footer disclosures (e.g. your PPRA Fidelity Fund Certificate number).
- **Social media:** links appear in the footer only when filled in.
- **Listing visibility:** under offer, sold and let.
- **About page:** your name, role, a short biography, the year founded and the areas you serve. These appear only once filled in.
- **Search engines:** the default description shown in Google.

Changes appear on the website immediately.

## Testimonials and team

- **Testimonials:** add only real quotes from clients **who agreed in writing** to be quoted. You must tick the consent box before you can publish. The homepage section appears automatically once one is published.
- **Team:** add agents who have agreed to be listed. They appear on the About page and can be chosen as the contact on a listing.

## Staff accounts (owner only)

**Users → Add a user** creates a login for a staff member (role *Staff*: listings and enquiries only). Give them the temporary password in person and ask them to change it. When someone leaves, choose **Disable**. Don't share your own login.

## Getting help

For anything technical (a page not loading, a password reset, changing the domain), contact your web developer and mention the time it happened and what you clicked.
