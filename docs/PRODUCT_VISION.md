# Product vision

## Purpose

Make people **notice** ERE, **trust** ERE, **explore properties** through ERE and **contact** ERE when they are ready to buy, sell or rent, and let the owner run it all without touching code.

Journey: **Discover ERE → Explore properties → Understand the market → Enquire or arrange a viewing → Become a client.**

## Audiences

| Audience | Needs | Where the site serves them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyers | Find suitable homes quickly, judge affordability, contact an agent easily | Search, Buy listings, property pages, bond calculator, WhatsApp/phone/enquiry |
| Tenants | Find rentals, see monthly cost | Rent listings (empty state + "tell us what you need" until rentals are published) |
| Sellers / homeowners | Understand the process, know their home's value, trust the agency | Sell page, valuation request, homepage seller CTA |
| The owner and staff | Publish listings, respond to leads fast, keep data safe | Admin dashboard |

## Core features (built)

Property search and filtering; property pages with gallery, facts, map with privacy levels, bond calculator and enquiry; valuation lead form; contact form; WhatsApp click-to-chat with property references; admin listings management with photo pipeline; enquiry pipeline (status, assignment, follow-up dates, notes, CSV export, deletion); site settings; testimonials and team that only appear once real content exists.

## Success measures (to track after launch)

- Number of valuation requests and property enquiries per month (dashboard counts).
- Time from enquiry to first contact (status changes from *New* to *Contacted*).
- Share of enquiries that come from WhatsApp vs forms (requires analytics; not installed. See TODO).
- Core Web Vitals in the field (LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1).
- Search impressions for "estate agent Parow" and similar (Google Search Console).

## Principles

Real data only: no fabricated listings, reviews, statistics or credentials. Everything visible is functional. Mobile-first and fast on South African connections. Content the owner hasn't confirmed is hidden, not invented.
